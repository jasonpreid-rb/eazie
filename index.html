Common questions

Do I need an account?

No. There is nothing to sign up for and no password to remember.

Where is my list stored?

On your own device, in your browser. We never see it. See the privacy policy.

Does it work without internet?

Yes. After your first visit it opens offline.

Can I use it on my phone and my computer?

Yes, but each device keeps its own separate list.

Does eazie send notifications?

No. Items that are due today, tomorrow or overdue pulse on screen when you open it.

Is it suitable for someone with dementia or Alzheimer's?

It is designed to be as simple as possible, but every person is different. Try it together first. eazie is an everyday organiser, not a medical device, so please do not rely on it for medication or anything safety-critical.

Can I add it to my home screen?

Yes. On Android tap Install when it appears. On iPhone tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.

Can I make the text bigger?

Yes. There are three text sizes, and eazie mode makes everything a little larger and calmer.
