Your list stays on your device

eazie saves to the browser on your own phone, tablet or computer. We do not collect your tasks.

After your first visit eazie opens without internet. Add it to your home screen and it launches like any other app.

Yes, but each device keeps its own separate list.

Your list is removed with it, because it is only stored there.
