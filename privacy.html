2. The short version

eazie keeps your projects and tasks only on your own device, in your browser's storage. We do not receive them, we cannot see them, and there is no account to create. If you accept analytics, we also count visits to this website using Google Analytics.

3. Hosting and server logs

This website is delivered by a third-party hosting provider that we have engaged to serve our files. When you open a page, the provider's servers temporarily record technical data such as your IP address, the time of the request and the page requested. This is needed to deliver and secure the site. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (our legitimate interest in a secure, working website). Logs are kept only for a short period set by the provider.

4. Data stored on your device

The app saves your projects and tasks, and your settings, in your browser's local storage. The settings are text size, work or personal view, eazie mode, whether you dismissed the install hint, and your cookie choice. A service worker keeps a copy of the app so that it can open without a connection. This is strictly necessary to provide the app you asked for (section 25(2) no. 2 TDDDG). None of it is sent to us. Clearing your browser data for this site deletes it, so we cannot restore it for you.

5. Google Analytics

Only if you press Accept on the banner, we use Google Analytics 4, provided by Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland. It sets cookies with names beginning _ga and records which pages are viewed, your approximate location, device and browser details, and two anonymous events: the app being installed, and an item being added (work or personal). The text of your projects and tasks is never sent. Google Analytics 4 does not log or store IP addresses. We have switched off Google signals and ad personalisation. Data is kept for no longer than 14 months.

Google may process data in the USA. Transfers rely on the EU-US Data Privacy Framework and, where applicable, standard contractual clauses. Legal basis: your consent, Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR and section 25(1) TDDDG. You can withdraw consent at any time with the Cookie settings link at the bottom of any page. Withdrawing does not affect processing that took place before.

6. Fonts

Fonts are hosted on our own server. Nothing is loaded from Google Fonts.

Contact us using the details above. You may also complain to a data protection supervisory authority, in particular in the EU member state where you live or work.

We may update this policy when the site or the law changes. The date at the top shows the latest version.
