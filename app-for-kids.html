A simple to-do app for kids who can read

eazie is a to-do list simple enough for children to use. If you can read, you can use it.

Try eazie now

Chores, homework and hobbies

Add a task, move it from New to Doing, then tick it off and watch it land in Done. Use a repeat for everyday jobs like feeding the cat or packing a school bag.

If you can read, you can use it

  • Big buttons with plain words.
  • Four clear steps: New, Doing, Waiting, Done.
  • Text size can be made bigger.

Private by design

There are no accounts, no chat and no sharing. A child's list stays on the device it was made on. Parents are welcome to set it up together.

Common questions

What age is eazie for?

Anyone who can read the words on the buttons.

Does my child need an account?

No. There is nothing to sign up for.

Can a parent see what my child writes?

Only on the same device. Nothing is sent anywhere.
